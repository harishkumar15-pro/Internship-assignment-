Who We Are

A Foundation Built on Belief & Action

NayePankh Foundation is a youth-led, non-profit organization working tirelessly to empower underprivileged children and young adults across India. Founded in 2020, we have grown from a small team of passionate volunteers into a nationwide movement.

Our name means "New Wings" — symbolizing our belief that every child, regardless of their background, deserves the opportunity to soar. We work directly in communities, schools, and digital spaces to deliver impactful, scalable programs.

🏫 Education 💼 Skill Development 🌿 Environment 💻 Digital Literacy ♀️ Women Empowerment 🏥 Health Awareness
Our Purpose

Mission, Vision & Values

We are driven by a singular belief: that opportunity should not be determined by the circumstances of one's birth.

🎯

Our Mission

To provide quality education, vocational training, and mentorship to underprivileged youth — enabling them to lead independent, dignified, and impactful lives.

🌟

Our Vision

An India where every child has equal access to education and opportunities — a nation where talent, not privilege, defines one's future.

💎

Our Values

Integrity, empathy, inclusivity, and accountability guide everything we do. We believe in community-first solutions and transparent impact reporting.

What We Do

Our Core Programs

Six focused initiatives, each designed to address a critical barrier in the journey of underprivileged youth.

📚

Shiksha Abhiyan

Free tutoring, study materials, and mentorship for students from Class 1–12, focused on government school children in rural and urban slums.

Education
🔧

Kaushal Vikas

Vocational training in tailoring, carpentry, mobile repair, and hospitality — equipping youth aged 16–25 with market-ready skills for employment.

Skill Development
🏥

Swasth Bharat

Community health camps, menstrual hygiene workshops, mental health awareness drives, and nutrition counseling reaching 200+ villages annually.

Health
💻

Digital Disha

Computer literacy, internet safety, and coding bootcamps for first-generation tech learners, partnered with 30+ corporate CSR initiatives.

Digital Literacy
♀️

Naari Shakti

Leadership workshops, legal rights awareness, self-defense training, and entrepreneurship support for young women aged 14–30 across 15 states.

Women Empowerment
🌿

Hariyali Mission

Youth-led tree plantation drives, clean-up campaigns, and eco-clubs in schools — instilling environmental responsibility from a young age.

Environment
